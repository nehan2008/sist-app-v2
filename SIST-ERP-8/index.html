sync_alt 1-Click Sync from Official ERP

Because Cloudflare Turnstile restricts verification to official Sathyabama domains, you can sync your live session directly from the portal in one click:

1 Log into Official ERP in your browser

Open erp.sathyabama.ac.in and sign in with Parent or Student credentials.

2 Use the Bookmarklet or 1-line Console command
bookmark Drag: 🔗 Sync SIST Portal (Drag to your Bookmarks bar)

Or paste this command into browser console (F12) on ERP:

window.location.href='http://localhost:3000/?token='+localStorage.getItem('Access-Token');
key Paste ERP Session Token

Enter the Access-Token from your browser session to immediately load your complete dashboard:

Tip: In ERP page console, type copy(localStorage.getItem('Access-Token')) to copy it to your clipboard.
